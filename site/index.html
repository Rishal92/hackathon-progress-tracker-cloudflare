How to use this tracker

Follow these steps to submit your progress, add your participant answers, and explore the optional stretch goal.

  1. Complete your GitHub username, repo URL, and live site URL in Participant details.
  2. Tick items in the Progress checklist as you finish each activity.
  3. Click Save progress to leaderboard to publish your latest status.
  4. Open Participant questions and answers to submit or update your question responses.
  5. Stretch goal: use the Fuse Blog API guide to integrate live blog content into your site.
